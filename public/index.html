        How to play

        1 · Enter

        Pick a starter — Bulbasaur, Charmander, Squirtle or Pikachu — a trainer name and a cap colour. You can play as a guest; sign in (Account panel) to keep your party, bag, points and balance.

        2 · Move

        WASD or arrows on desktop, the D-pad on phones. Everyone on this page is in the same world — you'll see other trainers walking around, and your active Pokémon follows you.

        3 · Catch

        Wild Pokémon hide in tall grass. Step onto one, then stop the moving marker in the green zone — gold is a perfect throw. You get three throws; Great and Ultra Balls from the shop raise your odds. 1 in 32 spawns is shiny (×3 points); legendaries are announced to everyone. Your party holds six — tap one in the Party panel to make it active.

        4 · Battle

        Walk next to a trainer and press Space (or Battle in the Nearby panel). Battles are turn-based, 25 seconds per turn: choose one of four moves or open the Bag — Potions heal, X Attack / X Defend buff you for three turns, and using an item takes your turn. The full type chart applies (water beats fire, ghost ignores normal, and so on). Winner +25 points, loser +5. Forfeiting or disconnecting counts as a loss.

        5 · Level up & evolve

        Your level is 1 + catches÷2 + wins÷2 and raises HP and damage. Starters evolve at Lv 4 and Lv 8, Pikachu at Lv 6, Magikarp at Lv 5, Eevee at Lv 5 into a random Eeveelution.

        6 · PokéCoins

        Every point you earn is also a PokéCoin. Spend them in Bag & shop on potions, X items and balls. Coins are game currency only — they're never bought or sold for tokens.

        7 · Accounts

        Connect wallet (Phantom / Solflare signs a message — no transaction, no fee) or create a smart wallet — a username + passphrase account that lives inside the arena, for players without a browser wallet. Either way your progress resumes when you come back.

        8 · Staking $POKEMON

        Signed in with a balance? Type a stake next to a nearby trainer's Battle button, or accept theirs. Both stakes are locked when the battle starts; the winner takes the pot minus a 4% fee. Stakes run from 1,000 to 5,000,000. Forfeit or disconnect loses your stake. Only stake what you can afford to lose — battles have luck in them.

        9 · Season prize pool

        75% of every fee goes into the pool. Every 24 hours it pays the season's top 3 by points — 50 / 30 / 20 % — and points reset. Catching, winning and staking all count.

        10 · Deposit & withdraw

        Deposit: send $POKEMON to your personal arena address (Account → Deposit) from any wallet or exchange, or let the site build the transfer for your wallet to sign. Credited within about a minute. Withdraw to any Solana address: minimum 1,000, up to 2,000,000 per day.
